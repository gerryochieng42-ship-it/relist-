About ReList

Better products shouldn't become waste just because they're pre-owned.

ReList is designed to make second-hand buying and selling feel simple, transparent and modern — whether you're browsing from New York, another U.S. city or abroad.

Safety first

Use good marketplace habits.

Meet in appropriate public places when possible, inspect products before paying, and never share sensitive financial information with strangers.
